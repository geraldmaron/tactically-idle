## Known failures in the existing people test

`src/content/people/people-content.test.ts` was written for the eight starters. With 39 authored jackets, three tests fail
and cannot pass at 100 officers. I did not weaken them.

1. "cover the eight starting officers and nobody else" expects exactly the eight starter personas.
2. "give each officer different markers, a different contradiction pattern and a different radio voice" needs 39 distinct
   noticed-first markers, patterns and habits. There are 9 noticed-first values, 9 patterns and 10 habits.
3. "give every starter a second and third page, each with a home district" requires exactly three pike officers. Batch
   B and D will decide the roster total, so the district counts are asserted at the roster level, not per file.

All three fail at their first assertion, so the rest of their bodies did not run on the full set. The batch's own versions
of the remaining checks (pronoun, role and trait words, radio grammar, page 2 and 3 present, place slot, home district is a
real district) passed in the temporary test, and the 31 Ready radio samples are all different (31 of 31).

Two further notes for whoever updates that test. The hobby-stem check only lists the eight starters, and
`toMatch(undefined)` passes for any other persona, so it does not cover this batch. The radio grammar test, the pronoun
test, the role and trait word test and the prose rule tests all pass on the full set of 39 jackets.
