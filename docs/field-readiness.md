# Field readiness and maintenance

## Standard radios

New live deployments automatically load one usable owned radio for each officer in the selected squads. Radios are standard kit, not an optional preset or quantity chore. The preparation screen shows the required count, exact serials and any shortage. A clearly priced purchase button can buy missing radios only when the player clicks it; changing squads recalculates the requirement. Existing operations retain their saved equipment, including older runs that deployed with fewer radios. Practice keeps its simulated equipment rules.

## Equipment manager

Hire the equipment manager through Gear or the Logistics development branch for $2,500 and 3 development points. This is a one-time department upgrade. Reusable equipment then wears 20% more slowly, both over time and when used; service prices are 25% lower, rounded upward to whole funding units. Consumables retain normal aging, expiry and use. Existing paid repairs are not repriced or refunded.

Automatic servicing starts paused. On Gear, the player can explicitly enable an hourly ceiling or pause it again. Each clock hour, while within the normal 24-hour unattended economy window, the manager considers idle equipment below 75% condition, worst first. It spends at most the chosen ceiling, keeps $1,000 in reserve and starts an automatic job only while fewer than two repairs are underway, counting manual jobs. The player can order additional manual repairs separately. Deployed/reserved equipment and consumables are excluded. Working radios needed by assigned officers stay available; failed radios and spare radios can be repaired. Pausing stops new repair orders; paid work finishes normally. Shift reports show the charges and serials sent for service.

## Equipment at the point of need

Preparation warnings can equip the required specialist gear and supplies from existing stock. In a live action sheet, a squad still staged outside before its first tactical decision can request a stores delivery. The preview lists its exact items and serials and the three-minute operation cost. Delivery uses normal pressure and civilian-safety time rules, reserves real units, and commits the operation, so it can no longer be cancelled as an untouched deployment. Further distinct deliveries are possible while still staged before a tactical decision; repeated requests never duplicate equipment. No gear is bought by delivery.

After a squad has moved into the operation, the sheet explains the restriction and offers an already equipped eligible squad when one is available. Equipment cannot replace a missing certification or another unmet action requirement. These logistics records are saved separately from tactical decisions, so they do not grant experience or consume an action.
